### Rate Limit
- **Limit:** Maximum 10 parallel audio creation requests. <br />
**What This Means:** You can send up to 10 concurrent requests total across all features combined for a given category. If you reach this limit, you must wait for some requests to complete before sending additional requests.
Requests to other categories are not affected by this limit and can be sent independently.

### Free Stem Presets
- **Limit:** During November 2026, [Extraction](/api-documentation/conversions/extraction) requests that use `stem_preset` are free on paid plans, up to **10 per UTC clock hour** and **100 per UTC day**. Requests past these limits are not rejected. They are charged at the usual rates. See the [free stem presets guide](/api-documentation/conversions/extraction-free-stems-guide).
